**Facebook Playbook:**
1. Join group, wait 24-48 hours before posting
2. First post: Educational ("THCA vs Delta-9: What retailers need to know")
3. Second post: Lab results / COA transparency
4. Third post: Soft pitch with wholesale pricing teaser
5. Reply to every "looking for supplier" post within 1 hour
6. Post consistently (3x/week minimum)

### Phase 3: Cold Calling & Direct Outreach (Weeks 2-Ongoing)

**Target List Building:**
- Use SmokeShops.com, Leafbuyer.com, Weedmaps.com directories
- Google Maps: search "smoke shop near [city]" for each target city
- Call stores directly, ask for owner or buyer
- Track all contacts in spreadsheet

**Phone Script:**
```
"Hi, I'm calling from OceanaHemp. We produce Farm Bill compliant 
THCA flower with full COAs. I'd love to send you a free sample 
so you can check out the quality. What's the best shipping address?"

If they're interested: "Great, I'll also include our wholesale 
pricing sheet. Do you have an email I can send the full catalog to?"
```

**Cold Call Schedule:**
- Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-4pm local time
- 30-50 calls per day
- Track: shop name, contact, interest level, follow-up date

### Phase 4: Tribal Outreach (Weeks 4-Ongoing)

**NC — Eastern Band of Cherokee:**
- Contact: Great Smoky Cannabis (tribal dispensary)
- Approach: Position as supplement to their supply chain
- Angle: Ocean wellness brand differentiator + competitive wholesale pricing
- Entry point: Tribal business development office

**AZ — Tribal Cannabis Compacts:**
- Targets: Tohono O'odham Nation, Pascua Yaqui Tribe, Gila River Indian Community
- Approach: Contact tribal economic development authorities
- Angle: Sovereign land = no state hemp restrictions apply
- Attend: AZ tribal economic development conferences

**FL — Seminole Tribe:**
- Targets: Seminole Tribe Economic Development, tribal convenience stores
- Approach: Hemp products for tribal retail (not cannabis dispensaries)
- Angle: CBD + THCA product line for tribal gas stations/stores

**All Tribal Outreach Tips:**
- Respect tribal sovereignty — approach through official tribal business channels
- Be patient — tribal decision cycles are longer than private retail
- Emphasize compliance documentation (COAs, Farm Bill compliance letters)
- Offer to present at tribal business councils
- Consider tribal white-label partnerships (their brand, your product)

## 9. RISK MONITORING

### Watch List (Legislation That Could Close Loophole)
- 2024 Farm Bill reauthorization (federal) — would redefine THC to include THCA
- FL 2025 legislative session — potential new hemp restrictions
- NC SB 596 reintroduction — likely in 2025 session
- AZ SB 1421 revival — possible
- TN SB 2013 negotiations ongoing
- DEA policy shifts — monitor for enforcement actions

### Contingency Plan
- If Farm Bill closes THCA loophole nationally: pivot to CBD-only wholesale, delta-8 where legal, and tribal markets (sovereign land exemptions)
- If individual states crack down: shift accounts to open states
- Keep COAs and compliance docs pristine — your best defense in any regulatory shift
