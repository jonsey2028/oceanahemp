## BRAND VALUES

1. **PURITY** — We never dilute, never cut, never settle. If it's not the purest form available, it doesn't leave our facility.

2. **INTEGRITY** — What's on the label is what's in the bottle. No fillers, no misleading claims, no fine print. We'd rather lose a sale than your trust.

3. **EFFECTIVENESS** — We make products that work. Period. Every formulation is backed by rigorous testing because results aren't optional — they're the whole point.

4. **TRANSPARENCY** — Full-lab results, honest sourcing, clear communication. We believe you should know exactly what you're putting in your body and where it came from.

5. **COMMUNITY** — From the smoke shop down the street to the tribal nations we partner with — we grow together. Our success is shared success.

---

## BRAND VOICE

**Tone:** Confident without arrogance. Knowledgeable without lecturing. Coastal — relaxed but razor-sharp on substance.

**We are:** Direct, honest, slightly irreverent. We talk like a friend who happens to know everything about hemp — not a corporation reading a script.

**We are NOT:** Preachy, vague, overly scientific (we simplify, never dumb down), or desperate to please.

**Key voice markers:**
- Short, declarative sentences
- Active voice, never passive
- Specifics over generalities ("third-party tested" not "high quality")
- Warm but firm — we have convictions and we state them
- Ocean references are natural, never forced
- Humor is dry and self-aware, never goofy

**Example voice:**
❌ "We strive to provide our valued customers with premium hemp products that may support overall wellness."
✅ "We make the purest hemp products we know how. If they weren't the best, we wouldn't sell them."
